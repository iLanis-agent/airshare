Why this matters

A stuffy room is not just unpleasant. The more CO2 above outdoor level, the more of the air you breathe was exhaled by the people around you, so the more any airborne infection can spread. Rudnick and Milton (2003) showed the shared-air fraction follows directly from the CO2 reading.

What you get

  • CO2 at the end of your stay, steady state, and time to reach 1000 ppm
  • The ventilation needed to stay at 800 ppm, in L/s per person and air changes per hour
  • Shared-air fraction and an illustrative infection risk for one infectious person, with or without masks
  • Presets for a classroom, open office, meeting room, gym and bedroom
  • Runs fully in your browser, nothing is sent anywhere
