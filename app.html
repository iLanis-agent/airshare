Model: well-mixed room, constant ventilation. CO2 rise = people x CO2 per person / fresh-air flow. Shared-air fraction f = (CO2 - outdoor) / 38,000 ppm (Rudnick & Milton 2003). Risk = 1 - exp(-f x infectious x quanta x hours / people). Quanta values, per-person CO2 rates and the mask factor are rough inputs, not measurements; real rooms are not perfectly mixed. CO2 bands (under 800 good, 800 to 1000 ok, 1000 to 1500 poor, over 1500 bad) are a rule of thumb. Educational estimate, not medical advice. Source: Rudnick & Milton, Indoor Air 2003
